TITLE
The Seven Worlds: A Soul's Journey to Brahmaloka | Hindu Cosmology Meditation Music

DESCRIPTION
A meditative journey through the seven higher worlds (lokas) of Hindu cosmology, from the banks of the Ganges to Satyaloka, the world of Brahma, and beyond, into pure light.

The path follows the Bhagavata Purana (Canto 2, Chapter 2, verses 19–31). A yogi raises his life-breath through six points of the body, leaves through the crown of the head, and travels upward along the Milky Way. He is purified in the fire of Agni Vaishvanara, passes the starry circle of Shishumara, and reaches Maharloka and Satyaloka. Then he moves through the coverings of earth, water, fire, air and ether into pure self-realization. The order and inhabitants of the worlds follow the Vishnu Purana (Book 2, Chapter 7).

Relax, breathe slowly, and travel upward with him.

ARTISTIC LICENSE
• In the texts the Kumaras appear unclothed. Here they wear simple garments.
• In Bhagavata Purana 2.2 the yogi does not return. The closing scene on the river is an artistic frame for the meditation.
• The music rises in register from world to world, a creative choice not taken from the texts.
