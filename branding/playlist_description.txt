A meditative journey upward through the seven worlds (lokas) of Hindu cosmology, one world at a time, from the earth to the abode of Brahma.

The series follows the Puranas themselves: the ascent of the yogi in the Bhagavata Purana (2.2) and the order of the worlds in the Vishnu Purana (2.7). Every scene is tied to a specific text, the sources are listed under each video, and anything added for the sake of art is marked as such.

Each world has its own music, built on a different Indian instrument and raga.

Painterly visuals in the style of Raja Ravi Varma and Indian miniature art, and calm instrumental music for meditation, relaxation and sleep. Visuals and music are created with AI tools, guided closely by the texts.
