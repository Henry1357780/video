Akasha Ganga — the "Ganges of the sky," the river of stars that ancient seers saw flowing across the heavens.

This channel offers meditative journeys into the inner and higher worlds described by the world's spiritual and esoteric traditions: the Puranas and Upanishads, yogic and mystical texts, and the sacred cosmologies of East and West.

Every journey is built on the sources themselves. Each scene follows a specific text, the texts are cited in the description, and anything added for the sake of art is marked as such. No invented "ancient secrets," only what the traditions actually record.

What you will find here:
• Slow, painterly visual journeys through sacred worlds
• Calm instrumental music for meditation, relaxation and sleep
• A doorway to the original texts, with references for further reading

Visuals and music are created with AI tools, guided closely by the texts.
