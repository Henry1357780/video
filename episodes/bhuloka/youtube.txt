DESCRIPTION
The first world of Hindu cosmology: Bhuloka, the earthly world, as the Puranas describe it.

At dawn on the Ganges a yogi sits in meditation, and the whole of Bhuloka opens before him. Bharatavarsha stretches from the Himalaya to the sea. The lotus-shaped island of Jambudvipa holds golden Mount Meru at its center. Around Meru stand four great mountains with their giant trees, and four lakes of milk, honey, sugarcane juice and pure water. The Jambu tree gives a river of juice and Jambunada gold. The city of Brahma crowns the summit of Meru. The Ganges descends from the heavens onto Meru and divides into four rivers. Seven island-rings and seven oceans surround it all, out to the Lokaloka mountain at the edge of the world, beyond which lies darkness. Then the soul begins its ascent toward the next world.

Part I of the series The Seven Worlds. Overview of the whole journey: "The Seven Worlds: A Soul's Journey to Brahmaloka".

SOURCES BY SCENE
• Bharatavarsha, from the ocean to the snowy mountains: Vishnu Purana 2.3
• Jambudvipa as a lotus with nine regions: Bhagavata Purana 5.16.5–6; Vishnu Purana 2.2
• Golden Mount Meru at the center, like the pericarp of a lotus: Bhagavata Purana 5.16.7
• The four mountains and their mango, jambu, kadamba and banyan trees: Bhagavata Purana 5.16.11–12
• The four lakes of milk, honey, sugarcane juice and water: Bhagavata Purana 5.16.13–14
• The Jambu tree, its river and Jambunada gold: Bhagavata Purana 5.16.19–21
• The city of Brahma on the summit of Meru and the eight cities of the guardians: Bhagavata Purana 5.16.28–29
• The Ganges descending onto Meru and dividing into four rivers: Bhagavata Purana 5.17.1–9
• The seven islands and seven oceans: Bhagavata Purana 5.20; Vishnu Purana 2.4
• The great lotus of Pushkaradvipa, the seat of Brahma: Bhagavata Purana 5.20.29
• Lokaloka and the darkness beyond: Bhagavata Purana 5.20.34–38; Bhuloka reaches as far as the sun and moon shine: Vishnu Purana 2.7
• The yogi's seat and the rising of the life-breath: Bhagavad Gita 6.11; Bhagavata Purana 2.2.19–22

ARTISTIC LICENSE
• The yogi's vision is a framing device. In the Bhagavata Purana, Shukadeva describes Bhu-mandala to King Parikshit.
• The ocean of milk surrounds Krauncha-dvipa in the Bhagavata Purana (5.20.18) but Shaka-dvipa in the Vishnu Purana (2.4), so the island is left unnamed.
